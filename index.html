✂️TikTok Clipper

Turn your own videos into TikTok-ready clips

Desktop app   For creators

TikTok Clipper is a desktop tool for creators. It downloads your own videos (e.g. from your YouTube channel), automatically cuts them into 1-minute vertical clips (9:16, 1080×1920, H.264/AAC), and publishes them to your own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.

Nothing is ever posted without you clicking “Publish”. You stay in control of captions and privacy for every post.

How it works

  1. Paste a link to a video you own; the app downloads and cuts it locally on your computer.
  2. Connect your TikTok account once, via the official TikTok login (OAuth).
  3. Add a caption, pick a privacy level, and publish your clips — one by one or all at once.
