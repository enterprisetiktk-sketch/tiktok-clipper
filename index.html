Turn your own videos into TikTok-ready clips

Desktop app   For creators

TikTok Clipper is a desktop tool for creators. It downloads your own videos, automatically cuts them into 1-minute vertical clips (9:16, 1080×1920, H.264/AAC), and publishes them to your own TikTok account using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. Nothing is ever posted without your authorization; you control captions and privacy.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we collect

The app runs entirely on your computer and operates no data-collecting backend. The only data are: settings and TikTok OAuth tokens stored locally on your device; the videos and clips you generate, stored locally; and your basic TikTok profile info (username, display name, avatar) fetched so the app can show which account is connected.

2. How data is used

Local data is used only to make the app work. Nothing is sold or shared. When you publish, your clip and caption are transmitted to TikTok because you asked for it; TikTok's privacy policy then applies.

3. Third-party services

The app integrates with TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) and downloads the videos you request; those services receive only the data required for the action you triggered.

4. Retention & deletion

Everything lives in the app folder on your computer. Delete the folder or use “Disconnect” and it is gone. You can revoke access at any time from your TikTok account settings.
