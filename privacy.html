Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What we collect

The app runs entirely on your computer. It does not operate a backend that collects personal data. The only data involved are:

  • Settings and connection tokens (TikTok OAuth tokens) stored locally on your device;
  • The videos and clips you generate, stored locally on your device;
  • Your basic TikTok profile info (username, display name, avatar) fetched from TikTok so the app can show which account is connected.

2. How data is used

Local data is used only to make the app work (remembering your connection, publishing the clips you asked to publish). Nothing is sold, shared or sent to the app publisher. When you publish, your clip and caption are transmitted to TikTok because you asked for that action; TikTok's own privacy policy then applies.

3. Third-party services

The app integrates with TikTok (Login Kit, Content Posting API) and downloads the videos you request. Those services receive only the data required to perform the action you triggered.

4. Data retention & deletion

Everything lives in the app folder on your computer. Delete the folder (or use “Disconnect”) and the data is gone. You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

5. Security

Tokens are kept on your device only. The local HTTPS endpoint is used solely for the TikTok login redirect on your machine.
